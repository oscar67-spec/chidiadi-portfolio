Anthropic's watermarking announcement is bad news for one specific group: people who pass off AI-generated text as their own.

That's the short version. The longer version is more interesting, and more useful if you write for a living.

## What watermarking actually changes

Watermarking makes AI-generated text detectable in a way that's hard to strip out. For publishers and platforms, it's a credibility tool. For "AI slop" operators, the ones flooding LinkedIn and Substack with generated filler, it's a problem.

## Why this is good news for real writers

If AI text is detectable, the premium on *actually writing* goes up. Specificity, lived experience, a real point of view: those are the things a watermark can't fake. The moat gets wider for people who do the work.

The lesson isn't "avoid AI." It's "don't let AI do the part that makes you worth reading."
