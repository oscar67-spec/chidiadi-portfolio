When you stare at a blank page, the instinct is to assume you've run out of things to say. You haven't. You've run out of *angles*.

The 4 Lenses Framework: take one event, anything you actually experienced, and run it through four different lenses to generate a week of publishing.

## The four lenses

- **Actionable:** Here's how. What's the practical takeaway, and what would you do differently next time?
- **Analytical:** Here's what. Strip away the emotion and break down the raw numbers or data.
- **Aspirational:** Here's the journey. What was the internal resistance or doubt before the breakthrough?
- **Anthropological:** Here's why. Zoom out to look at human behavior, patience, and industry incentives.

## Why it works

You don't need a brand-new idea every time you publish. You need one lived experience and multiple analytical lenses. The experience is the raw material; the lenses are the way you shape it into something useful for a specific reader.

This is the core of my content system, and it's the thing I teach founders who tell me they "have nothing to say."
