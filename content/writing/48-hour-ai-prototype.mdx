The old way: three days of research before a single cold message. The company, the person, the recent news, the product angles, the objection handling. By the time the research was done, the lead had gone cold.

The new way: a 15-minute brief, generated with AI, that compresses the parts that matter and skips the parts that don't.

## What changed

I stopped asking AI to "write me an outreach message." That produces slop. Instead, I built a small pipeline:

1. **Gather:** pull the prospect's site, posts, and public material into one place.
2. **Compress:** ask the model for the three most specific, non-obvious facts about them.
3. **Draft:** write the message around those facts, not around a template.

## Why it works

Cold outreach isn't won by volume. It's won by specificity. The AI doesn't make me smarter at research. It makes me *consistent* at it. I can now do in 15 minutes what used to take a weekend, and the message is better because it's grounded in something real.

The 48-hour prototype isn't about shipping fast. It's about closing the gap between knowing and doing.
