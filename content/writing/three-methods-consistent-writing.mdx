Everyone wants to write consistently. Almost no one does. The gap isn't talent: it's method.

Here are three methods I use to keep publishing when the motivation disappears.

## 1. Capture, don't create

Don't sit down to "write a post." Sit down to capture what you already noticed. Conversations, objections, support tickets, the thing a client said that annoyed you. Write from material, not from a blank page.

## 2. One idea per piece

A post isn't an essay. It's one observation, one argument, one "what if." The moment you try to say three things, you say none of them well, and you never finish.

## 3. Separate thinking from drafting

Batch the thinking. Outline on Monday, draft on Tuesday, edit on Wednesday. Never all in one sitting. The brain that generates and the brain that polishes are different brains, and switching between them mid-sentence is how drafts die.

## The point

Consistency isn't a personality trait. It's a system you can build. These three methods are the system.
