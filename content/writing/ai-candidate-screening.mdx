The biggest fear with automated hiring is filtering out your best applicant by mistake.

Hiring teams skip 40 hours of manual resume review and spend 100% of their time talking to their Elite tier. The reality is that hiring isn't an administrative contest to see who can read the most PDFs.

When you replace manual screening with intelligent filters, you eliminate reviewer fatigue, protect top talent from slipping through the cracks, and give candidates an objective evaluation.

## The system

I built an AI automation that:

1. **Ingests applications** as they come in (resume, cover letter, application form)
2. **Scores each candidate** against the role requirements using an LLM, not keyword matching, but actual fit assessment
3. **Flags edge cases** for human review instead of auto-rejecting
4. **Routes the top tier** directly to the hiring manager's calendar

## The result

AI won't replace human recruiters. But recruiters who master AI screening will run circles around teams still sorting resumes by hand.

The goal isn't to remove humans from hiring. It's to make sure humans spend their time talking to the right people. Not reading the same PDF for the 40th time.
