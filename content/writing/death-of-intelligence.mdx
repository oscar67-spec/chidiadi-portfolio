There's a version of the AI story where everything gets easier and nothing gets worse. This isn't that version.

The uncomfortable possibility: the more we outsource thinking to systems that produce plausible answers instantly, the less capable we become at producing them ourselves. Not because AI is malicious, but because that's what happens when a skill stops being practiced.

## The mechanism

Intelligence isn't a fixed tank. It's a muscle. It grows under load and atrophies without it. When the load disappears, when every hard question has a fluent, confident answer, the muscle doesn't get stronger. It gets bypassed.

## What we can do

This isn't a call to reject AI. It's a call to be deliberate about which thinking we keep. Some tasks should be automated. Some should be protected. The mistake is treating them the same.
