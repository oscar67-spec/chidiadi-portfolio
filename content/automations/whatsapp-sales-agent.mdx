## The problem

Sales conversations happen in real time. A prospect messages on WhatsApp at 9 PM. If you don't reply until morning, they've already bought from a competitor. But founders can't sit on WhatsApp all day.

## The solution

An AI sales agent that handles WhatsApp conversations end-to-end:

1. **Receives inbound messages** via WhatsApp Business API
2. **Qualifies the prospect:** budget, timeline, decision authority, fit
3. **Answers product questions** using a knowledge base of the company's offerings
4. **Handles objections** with proven sales responses
5. **Books calls** directly onto the sales calendar when a lead is qualified
6. **Hands off to a human** when the conversation gets complex or the prospect asks to talk to a person

## The stack

- n8n for workflow logic
- WhatsApp Business API for messaging
- OpenAI (GPT-4) for conversation intelligence
- Calendly for booking
- Pinecone/Weaviate for knowledge base retrieval (RAG)

## The result

Sales conversations that happen while you sleep. Qualified leads get booked calls automatically. Unqualified prospects get answered politely without wasting your time.
