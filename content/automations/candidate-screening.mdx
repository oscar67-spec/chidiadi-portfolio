## The problem

Hiring teams spend 40 hours a week reading resumes. The best candidates get buried under average ones. Reviewer fatigue sets in. Top talent slips through the cracks.

## The solution

An AI screening pipeline that:

1. **Ingests applications** (resume, cover letter, form responses)
2. **Scores each candidate** against role requirements using an LLM, assessing actual fit, not just keyword matching
3. **Flags edge cases** for human review instead of auto-rejecting good candidates
4. **Routes the top tier** directly to the hiring manager's calendar
5. **Sends rejection emails** to clear non-matches, so no candidate is left ghosted

## The stack

- n8n for workflow orchestration
- OpenAI (GPT) for candidate assessment
- Google Sheets / ATS integration for tracking
- Calendar API for interview booking
- Email for candidate communication

## The result

80% less time spent on manual screening. Hiring managers spend 100% of their time talking to elite candidates. Not reading the same PDF for the 40th time.

AI won't replace human recruiters. But recruiters who master AI screening will run circles around teams still sorting resumes by hand.
