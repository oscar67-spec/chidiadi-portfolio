## The problem

Finding local businesses for outreach means hours of manual searching on Google Maps, copying names, addresses, phone numbers, and websites into a spreadsheet. It's tedious, error-prone, and doesn't scale.

## The solution

An automated scraping workflow that:

1. **Takes a search query and location** (e.g., "dentists in Lagos," "coffee shops in Abuja")
2. **Scrapes Google Maps** for matching businesses: name, address, phone, website, rating, review count
3. **Cleans and deduplicates** the data
4. **Enriches with contact info** where available
5. **Exports to Google Sheets or CSV:** ready for outreach

## The stack

- n8n for orchestration
- Playwright / scraping tools for data extraction
- Google Sheets for output
- Data cleaning and deduplication logic

## The result

Local business intelligence delivered clean and structured. What used to take a day of manual copy-pasting now runs on schedule with zero manual effort.
