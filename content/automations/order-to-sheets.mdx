## The problem

Manually typing every new order into Google Sheets is slow and error-prone. For an ecommerce business, that's hours lost to data entry every week, and one typo means a shipment goes to the wrong address.

## The solution

A simple automation that:

1. **Captures new orders** as they come in (storefront, form, or email)
2. **Parses the order details:** customer, product, quantity, shipping address, total
3. **Routes each order to the right sheet** based on product category
4. **Logs it instantly:** no manual typing, no copy-pasting

## The stack

- n8n for workflow logic
- Google Sheets as the database
- Webhook or email trigger for new orders

## The result

Orders flow into the right sheets automatically. Zero manual data entry. Zero transcription errors. The team spends time fulfilling orders, not typing them.
